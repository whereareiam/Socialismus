Addons are plugins that extend Socialismus in some way without being loaded as modules through Socialismus itself.

Socialismus has two addon groups:

- official addons maintained by Socialismus developers
- community addons made by the community and not maintained by Socialismus developers

Community addons may integrate with other plugins, change runtime behavior, or come with their own release and support expectations.
Review them carefully before you install them.

## Addon discovery

Use these pages to discover addon plugins:

- [Official addons](./official/index.md)
- [Community addons](./community/index.md)

Some addons may be documented directly in this docs site.
Others may link out to separate documentation maintained elsewhere.
